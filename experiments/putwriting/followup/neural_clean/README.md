# Neural follow-up

The local clean search was stopped after 11 complete trials and 5 pruned trials, with one interrupted trial. No rolling results were generated from this study. The [trial status](results/hp_search/partial_status.json), [trial table](results/hp_search/trials_partial.csv), and [compressed log](tuning.log.gz) are retained. The reported backtest uses the [refit with the previous configuration](../neural_fallback/README.md).

The fresh Optuna study uses the first purged 8-year training and 2-year validation fold, with parameters fixed before all test folds. Early stopping and tuning use validation QLIKE of the uncorrected exponentiated log prediction. Tuning has a 35-minute session budget, with five minutes reserved for export, so the study may complete fewer than its target 25 trials. Check the exported study before describing its search size.

For each fitted model, fold and horizon, the Duan factor is the arithmetic mean of exp(log(y) - fitted_log_y) over the training sequences of that fitted model. This uses in-sample residuals and can be optimistic; validation and test outcomes do not enter the factor. The model is not refitted after validation-based early stopping. Corrected predictions are raw exponentiated predictions multiplied by the factor. Each checkpoint stores its factors in metadata; its predict method itself returns raw predictions, so consumers of checkpoints must apply the factors. Output tables retain both forms. Late origins without observed target outcomes have null target dates and outcomes as appropriate.

Input exclusion rules cover VIX3M, its term spread, and the three invalid Excel simple return columns. The panel hash and source fingerprints are recorded in the tuning and resume metadata. Models, selected features, training histories and forecast partials are retained under results. Runs use the cleaned panel prepared by ../panel.py. Logs record completion or errors.

This local supplemental search was deliberately interrupted after the prior-configuration fallback finished. It is incomplete: 11 trials completed, 5 were pruned, and the running trial was interrupted and marked failed. The SQLite database, trial histories, `trials_partial.csv`, `partial_status.json`, and tuning log preserve the evidence. No rolling run was launched from this incomplete search. The optional GPU notebook can run a fresh full search.
